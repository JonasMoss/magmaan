# Consolidated FIML study

The parent report is the reader-facing narrative and the parent runner owns
lane/diagnostic navigation. Lanes are parts of this experiment leaf, not
independent studies. Results symlinks point to their own root results tree.
Preserve each lane's original populations, target/null definition, seed rules,
failure accounting and historical metadata. Different estimators, mechanisms,
rank conditions or targets must not be pooled as one calibration result.

Navigation changed bootstrap/source fingerprints. Never relax resume guards
or relabel old checkpoints as compatible. Use a fresh absolute output directory
for verification; preserve every original result file and remote identifier.
Reports read only this study's results. Core/bindings own SEM primitives.

The prospective SEM design remains unfinished. Freeze source adaptations,
observation laws, estimator-level pseudo-nulls and numerical screening before
publication runs. Expected-H0 geometry is a diagnostic under nonnormal MAR;
observed-H0 sensitivity with the expected metric is the proposed primary.
The affine screen and completed multiplier comparisons are retained controls.
The two-stage and legacy-MLR lanes preserve older distinct paper designs, with
only smoke outputs locally. They are not queued grids or default evidence.

Track only the compact `results/overview/*.csv` evidence and its ignore file.
These frozen tables let the report render without the large ignored raw trees;
`scripts/summarize.R` derives them from original runs and records source hashes.
They retain separate designs, denominators and nonnormal-MAR stress regions;
they are not newly simulated evidence or a default-setting decision run.
